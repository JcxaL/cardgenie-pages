CardGenie Privacy Policy

Effective July 16, 2026 · Updated July 31, 2026

CardGenie is designed to work without an account, bank connection, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or CardGenie-operated server.

Data CardGenie handles

The cards, nicknames, benefit activity, reminders, preferences, and other information you enter are stored locally on your device using Apple system storage. CardGenie's widget receives a small on-device snapshot through an Apple App Group so it can display your next suggested credit. Local notifications are scheduled by iOS on your device.

CardGenie does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or use this information for tracking or advertising. It does not ask for full card numbers, bank credentials, or financial-account access. You may optionally enter a card's last four digits as a label; like everything else you enter, that label stays on your device.

Your operating-system settings may include CardGenie's local data in an encrypted device backup managed by Apple. CardGenie does not control or receive those backups.

External links

CardGenie can open issuer terms, this privacy policy, and the support website in your browser when you choose those links. Those sites are operated by their respective providers and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

You control the data stored by CardGenie. Individual cards and their history can be deleted in the app. Removing CardGenie and its data from your device removes its local app data, subject to any device backups you manage through Apple.

Changes

If CardGenie's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is distributed.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email leealvin2002@gmail.com. Do not include card numbers, bank credentials, or other sensitive financial information.
